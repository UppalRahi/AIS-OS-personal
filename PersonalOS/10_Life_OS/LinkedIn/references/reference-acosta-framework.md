# Lara Acosta LinkedIn Framework (Condensed)

**Model:** [Lara Acosta's framework for LinkedIn growth](https://www.linkedin.com/in/laraacosta/) — the Expertreneur playbook for personal branding, content, and conversion.

Lara Acosta teaches shifting from content creator to **domain authority**: proof-first content, profile engineered for conversion, disciplined engagement, and an owned-audience monetization path.

## Core thesis: Expertreneur

Prove you've done what you teach. Document, don't proclaim.

## SLAY post structure

| Letter | Meaning | Rule |
|--------|---------|------|
| S | Stop the scroll | Open with result, contradiction, or number — never context |
| L | Layer | One idea per paragraph/line |
| A | Anchor | Tie lesson to a real story with specifics |
| Y | You | One CTA: comment, follow, save, or click — pick one |

**Weak hook:** "LinkedIn personal branding is important if you want to grow in 2026."

**Strong hook:** "She built a following of hundreds of thousands on LinkedIn in under 2 years. Here is what she posted every single week."

## P.A.S. (conversion hooks)

1. **Problem** — clear pain (<10 words)
2. **Agitate** — make reader feel it + rehook for "see more"
3. **Solution** — direct answer

## Five-phase system

| Phase | Focus |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Profile conversion (headline, banner, featured, CTA) |
| 2 | Content flywheel (Rule of One, 80/20, proof-first, formatting) |
| 3 | Engagement protocol (pre-warm, post-launch, expert comments) |
| 4 | Monetization (newsletter, lead magnets, offer stack) |
| 5 | Authority stacking (collabs, thought-leader ads) |

## Content strategy

- **Rule of One:** one problem, one solution, one micro-niche
- **80/20 mix:** 80% tactical/educational, 20% personal (always tied to professional lesson)
- **Proof first:** screenshots, case studies, before/after, trench opinions
- **Funnel:** TOFU (reach) → MOFU (authority, bulk) → BOFU (case studies)

## Formatting

- Mobile-first: short lines, bullets, rhythm
- Image on every post, aligned with hook
- Carousels, infographics, screenshots = save-able

## Profile conversion (Phase 1)

- Headline: `I help X do Y with Z` → graduate to proof-first when ready
- Banner: social proof, offer, or testimonial
- Featured: 1 or 3 items (never 2) — lead magnet, newsletter, main offer
- CTA in pinned comment, not main post

## Engagement (Phase 3)

- 1-2 hr daily block centered on post time
- Post manually, never schedule
- Pre-warm: 10-20 comments before posting
- Post-launch: like own post + 3 self-comments + reply to all comments (first hour)
- 20-30 expert-led comments/day on others' posts

## Monetization path (Phase 4)

- DFY services → DIY products → DWY coaching
- Newsletter = owned audience
- Lead magnet post weekly with frictionless link
