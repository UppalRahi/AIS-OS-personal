# LinkedIn Voice Guardrails

Rahi's LinkedIn is **not** a sales channel. Brand grows through trust.

## Do

- Think out loud. Observations from real work — no client names unless Rahi approves.
- Share shifts in how he sees a problem.
- Ask genuine questions.
- Write like an introvert engineer finding words for systems thinking.
- Let expertise show through clarity, not credentials.

## Don't

- Flex client wins, revenue, or build times.
- Comment-keyword lead magnets.
- Proof-stacking openers (`5 BDRs, $2M company, 10 hours`).
- DM bait or service pitches.
- Clone creator energy from swipe research — borrow line rhythm only.

## Lara Acosta SLAY — Rahi's version

| Part | Job |
|------|-----|
| S | Honest observation or question |
| L | One thought per line |
| A | Personal moment from the work — no flex |
| Y | Open question or quiet close |

## Archetypes

Lord Hanuman (humility) + Doctorate (curiosity) over Founder-flex.
