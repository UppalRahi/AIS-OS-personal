# Client Project: HeyReach (LinkedIn Automation)

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Client Profile**: HeyReach is an agency-first LinkedIn outreach automation platform.
- **Sourcing Strategy**: Leveraged credibility as an official **n8n Creator** (showcased creator profile at `n8n.io/creators/rahiuppal/`). Cold DMed Nick (Founder of HeyReach) offering a free custom n8n workflow. Nick passed the conversation to Bojana (Affiliate & Partnership manager). Signed an NDA and scoped paid template creation.
- **Engagement Type**: Fixed-price asset delivery.

---

## 2. The Bottleneck (Friction Point)

- **Old Process**: HeyReach users had to manually map APIs to construct custom workflows in n8n, creating a barrier to adoption for non-technical users.
- **Goal**: HeyReach wanted to publish official, pre-built n8n templates to streamline user onboarding and drive integrations.

---

## 3. The Machine (Technical Architecture)

- **Workflow Orchestration**: **n8n** template creation.
- **APIs & Tools Integrated**: **HeyReach API** endpoints (campaigns, webhook registrations, lead updates).

### Process Map / Logic Flow
1. **Trigger**: User installs the HeyReach integration template in n8n.
2. **Action 1**: Template maps HeyReach payloads and authenticates requests.
3. **Action 2**: Programmatic synchronization of leads or campaign statistics.
4. **Destination**: Public n8n template library and HeyReach integration resources.

---

## 4. The Money (Business Impact & ROI)

- **Execution Speed**: High-margin, low-effort work due to existing authority.
- **Time/Cost Savings**: Shipped templates instantly; required very little development time because of previous expertise with the HeyReach API.
- **Project Economics**:
  - Total Hours Spent: **2 hours**
  - Total Earnings: **$290 USD**
  - Effective Hourly Rate: **$145 / hour**

---

## 5. High-Leverage Learning / Post-Mortem

- **What worked?**: Credibility and authority (being an official "n8n Creator") serve as a powerful moat. A cold DM with a free sample tool can instantly open doors with SaaS founders.
- **Takeaway**: Asset creation (like templates or reusable modules) yields much higher effective hourly rates than custom services.
