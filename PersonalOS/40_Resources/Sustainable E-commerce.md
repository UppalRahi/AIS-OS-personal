# Resource: Sustainable E-commerce

This note summarizes sustainable market trends and operations, drawing from experience at **Ecoyaan** (DPIIT-registered eco-friendly e-commerce marketplace).

---

## 1. The Ecoyaan Case Study

- **Company Overview**: India's first AI-powered marketplace for sustainable and healthy products. Uses a proprietary vetting engine called **Ecoverify™** to assess packaging, ingredients, supply chain transparency, and certifications.
- **The Problem**: Greenwashing makes it difficult for conscious consumers to trust eco-friendly claims, creating high friction in purchasing decisions.
- **Operational Improvements**: By automating the management of seller onboarding data sheets with GPT and Python, Rahi improved seller conversions by **20%** and enhanced data accuracy, streamlining the integration pipeline.

---

## 2. Key Learnings & Strategy

### Combatting Greenwashing
- **Strategy**: Move away from vague marketing claims ("natural," "green," "eco") toward structured, verifiable data attributes (e.g., carbon offsets, plastic-free certifications, ingredient transparency).
- **Automation Potential**: Use LLMs to read product spec sheets, compare them against standard environmental databases, and automatically flag high-risk or unverifiable claims.

### Onboarding Automation
- **The Friction**: Green sellers are often small businesses with messy product data, lack of standardized catalog templates, and slow response times.
- **The Cure**: Build self-service onboarding portals that use AI to ingest unstructured PDF lists/catalogs, categorize products automatically, write SEO-optimized sustainable copy, and populate e-commerce databases (Shopify/Magento/WooCommerce) instantly.
